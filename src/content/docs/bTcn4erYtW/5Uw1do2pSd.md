**Meeting Date:** 15th July
**Location:** Fiona's House
**Key Participants:** Fiona (Chair), Tess (Litter Pick Lead), Colette (Resident/Committee), Grace (Gracewell/Council), and other local residents.


---

#### **1. Community Event Debrief (June Event)**

The recent community event was deemed a significant success, with an estimated **200 attendees**.

* **Engagement Metrics:** The group used ice cream tokens (150+ distributed) to capture resident postcodes, allowing for geographical analysis of participation.
* **Operational Successes:** The vegan food options were well-received, and the music level was appropriate for conversation while maintaining atmosphere. Local wildlife engagement, such as the Hedgehog/iNaturalist stall, was also popular.
* **Lessons for Future Events:**
  * **Volunteer Shortage:** There was a clear need for more volunteers to man stalls, as committee members were often "overwhelmed" by administrative tasks.
  * **Logistics:** Suggestions were made to centralise the DJ for better sound distribution and to improve the briefing for road closures to prevent resident backlogs.

#### **2. Financial & Administrative Updates**

The group reviewed the budget and pending payments to finalise the recent event's accounts.

* **Payments:** Outstanding invoices include Unity Arts, additional printing, and catering/refreshments.
* **Budgeting:** Initial planning for the **2025 funding bid** has commenced. Future costs were estimated for the newsletter (£260), AGM food (£350), and bulb planting (£320).
* **Banking:** The group is in the process of transitioning to a new bank account over the summer period.

#### **3. Autumn & Winter Strategic Calendar**

The committee established a timeline for the remainder of the year to ensure adequate publicity and preparation.

* **Litter Pick:** Scheduled for **Sunday, 19th July** (commencing 10:00 am) to mark the start of the summer holidays.
* **Autumn Newsletter:** A double-sized newsletter will be prepared. Content must be ready by **10th October** for printing on the **12th October**. This will include a QR code for a community survey.
* **AGM & Nature Event:** Set for **Saturday, 7th November** (1:00 pm – 5:00 pm).
  * **1:00 pm:** Community Lunch.
  * **2:00 pm:** AGM and iNaturalist/Nature presentation.
  * **3:00 pm:** Advent crafting workshop.

#### **4. Community Engagement & Digital Management**

* **WhatsApp Group:** The group has seen an increase in members. While generally productive, the committee emphasized the importance of maintaining a friendly environment and will monitor for inappropriate language or repetitive negative posts.
* **Advent Window Displays:** A call for themes will be issued via the newsletter and a poll on WhatsApp. Past themes include "Birds" and "The Three Wise Men of Barlow".
* **Local Outreach:** There is a desire to improve engagement with residents in newer developments and surrounding areas like Hardy Lane.
