**BHNG Summer community event**

> **Date:** Saturday 6 June
**Location:** Mottram Road green space
**Host:** Barlow Hall Neighbourhood Group
**Proposed duration:** 1:00 – 4:00 pm

# Event overview

The BHNG summer community event is a family-friendly, inclusive gathering designed to bring local residents together and build social connection across the neighbourhood. 


The event will provide opportunities for connection, wellbeing, learning, and fun through activities, entertainment, and refreshments in a relaxed and welcoming environment.


The event aims to strengthen community relationships, increase engagement with BHNG, and create a positive shared experience for residents of all ages.

# Background

Intro about BHNG… who we are, what we do, how to get involved (copy from newsletter)

# Objectives


1. Provide a fun and enjoyable event for local residents
2. Encourage community connection
3. Encourage greater involvement of residents in BHNG activities
4. Promote local groups, services, and initiatives

# Target audience

All BHNG residents


# BHNG information and engagement stall


1. Information about the group and how to get involved (sign-up sheet), promote the WhatsApp chat and sub-groups (QR code)
2. Information on local activities, groups, events that residents can join (Chorlton booklet recently produced)
3. A map for people to say what they do/ don't like about the area
4. Asking for ideas on people want to see and do in the area
5. Ask people on the WhatsApp group to tell their neighbours about the group and the event
6. Info on committee members

* Running activities or demonstrations

# Promotion and communication

The event will be promoted through:

* Posters on community noticeboards around the Barlow Hall estate 
* Barlow Hall chat WhatsApp group
* BHNG newsletter; distributed to every home on the estate
* BHNG website and social media channels
* Word-of-mouth; ask people on the WhatsApp group to tell their neighbours about the group and the event

# Evaluation and outcomes

Success will be measured through:

* Attendance numbers
* Resident feedback (informal conversations or short surveys)
* Level of engagement with stalls and activities
* Overall atmosphere and community participation

Feedback will help inform future community events.
